Offer Clarity Checklist

A one-page worksheet for freelancers, creators, coaches, and consultants before they publish a service page, workshop page, or waitlist.

How to use this: answer the prompts before building the landing page. If one section is vague, the page will probably feel vague too.

1. The core offer

I can explain the offer in one sentence. Example: “I help solo consultants turn messy client onboarding into a simple 5-step system.”
I know the buyer’s current pain. Use plain language they would actually say, not internal jargon.
I know the next step I want visitors to take. Book a call, join a waitlist, buy, email, download, or reply.

2. The page ingredients

HeadlineA clear outcome or situation, not a clever slogan.
Value blocksThree reasons the offer is useful, specific, and believable.
CTAOne visible action repeated in the hero and near the end.
FAQAnswers to objections: fit, timing, deliverables, risk, and limitations.

3. Quick worksheet

PromptYour answer
Who is this for?
What problem are they trying to solve?
What changes after they use or buy this?
What should they do next?
What is not included?
