Landing page copy examples you can adapt before redesigning.

Three fictional before-and-after examples showing how a vague offer becomes a clearer hero, CTA, proof block, and lead magnet angle.

How to use this

Do not copy the words blindly. Copy the decision logic.

Each example answers four questions a landing page must make obvious: who it is for, what problem it solves, what the visitor should do next, and what low-friction resource helps them move forward.

Example 1: consultant offer

Before

We help businesses grow faster.

CTA: Contact us today.

Problem: too broad. It says nothing about the buyer, the specific pain, or the first step.

After

Find the revenue leaks in your client onboarding before hiring another salesperson.

CTA: Book a 20-minute onboarding audit.

Lead magnet: 12-point client onboarding leak checklist.

Why it works

The rewrite names the situation, the painful alternative, and a concrete diagnostic next step. It does not promise guaranteed revenue.

Example 2: creator workshop

Before

Join my productivity workshop.

CTA: Sign up.

Problem: it sounds generic and asks for commitment before explaining the outcome.

After

Turn your scattered content ideas into a 30-day publishing plan in one afternoon.

CTA: Join the next workshop waitlist.

Lead magnet: 30-day content map worksheet.

Why it works

The page can now sell a specific transformation and use the worksheet as a useful sample of the workshop method.

Example 3: coaching offer

Before

Career coaching for professionals.

CTA: Schedule a call.

Problem: the audience is huge, the pain is unclear, and the call feels too heavy for a cold visitor.

After

Make your next career move without rewriting your life from scratch.

CTA: Take the 5-question fit check.

Lead magnet: career reset readiness checklist.

Why it works

The new copy gives cautious visitors a smaller first step and frames the call around fit, not pressure.

Quick test

The 5-second hero check.

Show your hero section to someone for five seconds, hide it, then ask: who is this for, what do they get, and what should they do next? If they cannot answer, fix the hero before changing colors or layouts.
