Scope score

Add one point for each item you need.

A score under 4 usually fits a lean landing page. A score over 6 is probably a broader website or marketing project, not a fast validation page.

  • Custom visual design beyond a clean layout.
  • More than one page or multiple audience segments.
  • Copywriting from scratch with no existing offer notes.
  • Payment, booking, CRM, email, or analytics integrations.
  • CMS/blog editing after launch.
  • Brand identity, logo, icons, or illustrations.
  • SEO research, migration, or content strategy.
  • Multiple stakeholder reviews or approval rounds.
Decision guide

Do not buy a bigger project before these are clear.

Offer

Can you describe it in one sentence?

If not, spend time on the offer before spending money on design.

Audience

Can you name the buyer and moment?

“Small businesses” is too broad. “Solo consultants launching a paid audit” is usable.

CTA

What should the visitor do next?

Email, book, download, join a waitlist, or buy. One primary action is enough.

Proof

What can you show honestly?

Use examples, screenshots, process, credentials, or before/after work. Never invent trust signals.
