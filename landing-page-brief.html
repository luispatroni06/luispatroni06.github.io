Landing Page Brief Template

Use this brief before hiring someone, writing copy, or opening a page builder. The goal is to remove guesswork: one audience, one offer, one next step.

Best for: freelancers, creators, coaches, and consultants preparing a service page, workshop page, waitlist, or simple lead magnet funnel.

1. Decide the job of the page

Primary actionChoose one: book a call, join a waitlist, download a resource, email you, or buy.
Visitor stageAre they cold, warm, referred, or already familiar with you?
Proof levelList what you can honestly show: examples, process, credentials, case notes, or a sample asset.
BoundaryState what the offer does not include so the page does not overpromise.

2. Answer these before writing copy

Who exactly is this for?

Example: independent career coaches selling a first paid workshop, not “everyone who needs marketing.”

What problem are they trying to solve now?

Use words they would recognize from their own situation.

What changes after they take action?

Describe a realistic next state, not a guaranteed financial result.

Why should they trust this offer?

Use honest proof: clear process, samples, limitations, relevant experience, or a small low-risk first step.

What might stop them?

Turn the top objections into FAQ answers: timing, fit, price, deliverables, revisions, risk.
