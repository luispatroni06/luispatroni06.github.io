The outline

Six sections are enough for a first service page.

01 · Hero

Audience + outcome + CTA

Write one sentence that says who this is for, what result they want, and the action they should take.

02 · Problem

Name the buying moment

List the pains that make the visitor look for help now, not generic industry problems.

03 · Offer

Make the scope concrete

Explain what they get, what is not included, timeline, inputs required, and the next step after they click.

04 · Proof

Use honest evidence

Use testimonials, screenshots, examples, process notes, or before/after samples. Do not invent logos or results.

05 · FAQ

Handle real objections

Answer fit, price, timeline, guarantees, what you need from the buyer, and what happens if the first draft is not perfect.

06 · CTA repeat

End with one action

Repeat the primary CTA and, if the ask is high-friction, add a lighter resource such as a checklist or brief template.

Fill-in prompt

Copy this before writing the page.

For [audience] who are struggling with [specific problem], I help them get [specific outcome] by delivering [service scope]. The next step is [CTA]. It is a good fit when [fit criteria] and not a fit when [exclusion].

Quick quality check

  • Can a stranger tell who the page is for in 5 seconds?
  • Does the CTA match the visitor's readiness?
  • Is the offer scope specific enough to buy?
  • Is every claim backed by visible evidence?
